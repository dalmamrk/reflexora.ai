Application Areas

The REFLEXORA portfolio covers robotics, mobility, autonomous systems, energy harvesting, energy storage, sensing, edge AI, and IoT. Current application studies include local PV shadow response, module-local battery reflex balancing, Spatial AI event-to-action contracts, and bio-inspired safety-critical edge systems.

Robotics and Humanoids

Local impact, torque, contact and balance reflexes that remain active while higher AI performs perception and planning.

Automotive and Autonomous Systems

Fast current, voltage and temperature protection combined with predictive energy management and higher-level autonomy.

Energy Harvesting

Local photovoltaic and harvester reflexes that protect energy capture before slower optimization loops respond.

Energy Storage

Cell-level permissions, balancing routes and fault isolation under Battery Management System supervision.

Edge and IoT

Always-on event detection, selective wake-up and compressed communication for energy-constrained devices.
